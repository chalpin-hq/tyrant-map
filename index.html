Welcome to the Tyrant Map Planner

Nothing here saves automatically. Everything you place lives only in this browser tab until you export it.

  • ↓ Export downloads a file with everything currently on the map (terrain, Dominions, and your Hub/Outpost/City). Keep that file so you don't lose your layout, or send it to a guildmate to share what you've got.
  • ↑ Import loads a file someone exported (including your own earlier export), replacing what's currently shown.
  • Click a marker without dragging it to rename or recolour it. Different colours per guild/alliance make an easy "war map".
  • Click-drag a marker to move it. Pick ✕ Erase, then click a marker to remove it. The three Watchtowers in Core can be dragged too, so you can reposition them after the Throne changes hands.
  • Pan by dragging empty space; zoom with the scroll wheel or a pinch.
